How to read this
  • The ceiling is Belanja K/L in APBN 2026 (Rp1.510,5 T). In Reallocate mode the total never moves.
  • In Add spending mode, extra pagu raises Belanja Negara and the deficit one-for-one, capped at 3% of GDP (UU 17/2003). GDP is the one implied by the APBN 2026 deficit design.
  • Pagu can't drop below what a K/L has already spent; realisation can only rise from its September position.
Demo notice: ministry and agency figures are simulated (APBN_2026_Dummy.xlsx) and are not official Ministry of Finance data.
